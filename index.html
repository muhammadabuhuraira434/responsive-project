About the shop

We started as a two-person operation out of a rented kitchen in 2019. Still small, still stubborn about doing things the slow way — grinding beans to order and baking everything on site.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am till 3pm. Closed Mondays (that's when we restock).
